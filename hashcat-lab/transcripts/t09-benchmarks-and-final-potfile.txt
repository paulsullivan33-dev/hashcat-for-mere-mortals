Same two processors, both times. MD5 is about 224,000 times faster
to test than sha256crypt (124,200,000 / 554) — that gap is not hashcat
being clever or slow; it is the two hash designs doing exactly what
they were built to do. (A repeat benchmark run ten minutes earlier
read 113.7 MH/s and 499 H/s: the machine is shared, so treat every
speed here as a range, not a constant.)
